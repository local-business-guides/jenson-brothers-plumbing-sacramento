A quick checklist before you choose

Use reviews as a starting point, then check the practical details that matter to your household. Before arranging service, consider asking:

  • Does the company handle the repair, maintenance, or installation I need?
  • Can someone explain the next steps in clear language?
  • How can I reach the company if the issue needs emergency attention?
  • What should I note or do before the plumbing visit?

Avoid choosing based only on a single glowing or negative comment. Don’t assume a review describes the same fault, home, or service you’re dealing with. Also be cautious about treating a reviewer’s suggested fix as instructions for your own plumbing. If you’re unsure about a repair, ask a professional rather than taking equipment apart or trying a complicated fix yourself.
