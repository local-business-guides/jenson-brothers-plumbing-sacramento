What to discuss before work begins

A plumbing visit may involve inspecting the reported issue, explaining the proposed repair or installation, and discussing what happens next. The scope depends on the problem, so describe what you've noticed rather than requesting a specific fix before it has been assessed.

A short checklist can keep the call focused:
- Share your Sacramento address and a working callback number.
- Describe when the problem began and what you can observe.
- Mention any steps you've already taken, such as shutting off water.
- Ask about license details, the work scope, and what to prepare.

Avoid assuming that a quick symptom points to one particular cause. Also, don't agree to work you don't understand. Ask for a plain explanation of the proposed service before it starts.
